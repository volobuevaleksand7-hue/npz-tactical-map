Croatia's JANAF pipeline leaks 500 cubic metres of oil, pumping halted

On 6 November the JANAF pipeline was damaged during refurbishment work on the Sisak–Slavonski Brod section near the village of Prvča close to Nova Gradiška; the company estimates about 500 cubic metres of oil leaked, with roughly 200 cubic metres recovered by 7 November. Pumping was halted by closing the Okučani and Lužani block stations and the damage was sealed; according to JANAF there is no threat to residents or drinking water and oil recovery will continue for several days.

Effect on fuel. Crude transport on the Sisak–Slavonski Brod section was halted; according to the company, the leak was contained and oil recovery continues.

Qatar's prime minister heads to Washington with Iranian proposal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz

On 9 November Qatar's prime minister headed to the White House with an Iranian proposal for an interim deal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz. According to two diplomatic sources cited by Israeli media, Tehran agreed to reopen the strait and halt attacks on Gulf tankers in exchange for lifting the naval and economic blockade and unfreezing $8 billion in Qatar. Neither government confirmed the terms; US Secretary of State Marco Rubio sharply criticised Iran's demands and said the sides were far from a breakthrough, while US President Donald Trump called renewed strikes unlikely if a strong agreement is reached.

Effect on fuel. Brent fell 3.6% to $94 a barrel, capping a 16% weekly decline, the largest since the start of the COVID-19 pandemic.

Iraq agrees with US on oil exports through Hormuz via a corridor off Oman

On 8 November Iraqi Foreign Minister Fuad Hussein said in an Al Jazeera interview that Baghdad had reached understandings with the United States to export Iraqi oil through the Strait of Hormuz via a maritime corridor off the coast of Oman. He said Iraq's losses from the war and export disruptions had reached $60 billion. According to Iraqi energy officials, exports rose to about 3.5 million barrels per day in October from 2.65 million in September; there is no US confirmation of the deal's terms.

Effect on fuel. The arrangement gives Iraqi exports a route through the corridor off Oman; in October Iraq's exports rose to 3.5 million barrels per day from 2.65 million in September.

reported, single source Middle East · 7 November 2026

UKMTO: tanker fire and projectile hit reported off Musandam within an hour

On 8 November UKMTO reported two incidents off the Musandam coast. At about 19:20 UTC on 7 November a tanker caught fire 8 nautical miles northwest of Khasab, Oman, and the crew was evacuated; at about 20:20 UTC on 7 November a tanker outbound through the Strait of Hormuz was struck by an unknown projectile, with the crew safe. The cause of the fire was not stated, both UKMTO reports were time-late and are being investigated, and there is no independent confirmation of attribution.

Effect on fuel. Impact not assessed.
