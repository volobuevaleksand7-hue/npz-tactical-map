reported, single source Africa · 2 November 2026

Malawi: queues at filling stations amid petrol shortage and hard-currency shortfall for imports

According to The Guardian of 2 November, drivers in Malawi are waiting for petrol for days: a minibus driver in Zomba reportedly waited nine days at the Meru station, and at the Petroda station in Lilongwe daily capacity fell from 30,000 to 5,000 litres. According to the National Oil Company of Malawi, the cause is a shortage of hard currency to pay for imports; banks reportedly refuse letters of credit and tankers are held up at regional ports. The escalation began in late October.

Effect on fuel. The shortage of hard currency reportedly delays tankers at regional ports and reduces petrol supplies in the country.

reported, single source Middle East · 2 November 2026

According to US statements, limited strikes on Qeshm Island on 2 November were a response to threats to shipping

On 2 November, according to US officials, US forces carried out limited strikes on targets on Qeshm Island, Iran, including missile launchers. The officials described the strikes as defensive, a response to Iranian use of surface-to-air missiles against US forces, and not a resumption of large-scale combat. Iranian state media reported five explosions on the island. Casualties and damage have not been established. On 4 November President Trump confirmed a strike on Qeshm, described it as minor, and said the target was an anti-aircraft facility.

Effect on fuel. Impact not assessed.
