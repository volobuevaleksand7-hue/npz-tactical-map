confirmed East Asia · 26 November 2026

Explosion and fire at Bangchak refinery in Bangkok: nine injured, all units shut

On 26 November an explosion and fire occurred at the Bangchak refinery in Bangkok's Phra Khanong district; at least nine people were injured, four of them seriously, according to the city's Erawan Emergency Medical Centre. Bangchak halted all production units; preliminary findings point to the hydrocracking unit. Air quality measurements within a 3-4 km radius were within normal ranges, and residents were urged to evacuate.

Effect on fuel. A full shutdown of the refinery's units suspends fuel output at the site; the company did not say when operations would resume.

confirmed Middle East · 26 November 2026

Projectile hits Jazan refinery: one worker killed, some operations halted

On 26 November an aerial projectile hit the refinery in Saudi Arabia's Jazan on the Red Sea coast, killing one worker and injuring several others, starting a fire and temporarily halting some operations, the Saudi Ministry of Energy said. The ministry did not identify who carried out the strike; the Saudi-led coalition said the Houthis attacked the refinery. The strike came a day after Houthi claims of attacks on Ras Tanura and the ports of Jeddah and Jazan.

Effect on fuel. The ministry reported a temporary halt of some operations; the scale of lost refining output was not disclosed, so the impact on regional fuel supply has not been assessed.

confirmed Latin America · 26 November 2026

Fire at Venezuela's Amuay refinery: workers evacuated, distillation unit halted

On 26 November a fire broke out in a trench holding residual oil products at Venezuela's largest refinery, Amuay in Falcon state; workers were evacuated, the blaze was extinguished and no injuries were reported, PDVSA said. According to one worker, the distillation unit was temporarily shut down, the only unit currently operating at the plant; another worker said the fire started in a naphtha line. PDVSA said the incident did not affect domestic fuel distribution and did not address the unit's status or the cause.

Effect on fuel. The shutdown of Amuay's only operating unit (plant capacity 645,000 barrels per day, actual throughput about 67,000) curbs refined product output, although PDVSA denies any effect on domestic supplies.

reported, single source Europe · 26 November 2026

Bloomberg: OPEC+ leans toward keeping January quotas unchanged before November 29 meeting

OPEC+ is considering keeping current crude production quotas in place through January 2027 and will discuss the proposal at an online meeting on November 29, Bloomberg reported. Targets for next year have not been finalised; some members have yet to return curtailed volumes, and production disruptions in the Gulf region have kept higher quotas from translating into actual supply increases. The talks were complicated after Iraq challenged the assessment of its production capacity.

Effect on fuel. If quotas are left unchanged, OPEC+ would add no extra oil supply in January; amid Gulf disruptions this supports prices.

reported, single source Africa · 26 November 2026

Sierra Leone: fuel queues and higher black-market prices ahead of census

A week before census night, fuel shortages have returned in Sierra Leone: queues have formed in Freetown, Bo and other parts of the country, some pumps are closed and black-market prices are higher, The Patriotic Vanguard newspaper reported, citing sources in the Ministry of Information. The government acknowledged the disruptions and said it does not plan to raise the regulated retail price for the rest of 2026. Local outlets link the supply delays to a shortage of foreign currency to pay for imports.

Effect on fuel. Import disruptions constrain retail petrol and diesel supplies and push prices up outside the regulated market.
