OPEC+: seven countries keep December production targets unchanged

At a meeting on 1 November, the seven OPEC+ countries decided to keep December output at the level required for September, about 31 million barrels per day. The next meeting is scheduled for 29 November. According to The National, actual output in Gulf countries remains below target levels because of export disruptions.

Effect on fuel. The decision adds no supply beyond actual production, which reportedly remains below target levels.

Iran's Strait of Hormuz authority announces measures against Sinokor tankers

On 27 October, according to Bloomberg, Iran's Persian Gulf Strait Authority said it would take legal action against tankers of the Sinokor company for violations of transit rules, and reported enforcement measures against at least seven vessels. No independent confirmation is available in the reviewed materials.

Effect on fuel. Impact not assessed.
