confirmed Europe · 19 November 2026

IEA calls Strait of Hormuz crisis largest energy supply shock in history

On 19 November the IEA published its World Energy Outlook 2026, calling the current crisis the largest energy supply shock in history; according to the agency, 20 million barrels per day of oil passed through the Strait of Hormuz before the crisis, and in a case of prolonged shipping risks that volume could fall to 8.5 million barrels per day by 2030 and to under 2 million by 2035.

Effect on fuel. The IEA expects bypass pipelines and demand displacement to reduce dependence on Hormuz, but even in that scenario the Middle East would remain the largest oil exporter.

confirmed North America · 19 November 2026

US gasoline prices fall 50.5 cents in three weeks, biggest drop since 2008

According to AAA data cited by CNN on 19 November, the US national average gasoline price fell 50.5 cents from 29 October to USD 3.72 per gallon, the lowest since 16 March; the decline was the steepest three-week drop since 2008. Prices have fallen for 26 consecutive days.

Effect on fuel. A 12% drop in US retail gasoline prices over three weeks lowers fuel costs for consumers.

confirmed East Asia · 18 November 2026

Explosion at training refinery in Cepu, Indonesia, kills one worker

On 18 November an explosion occurred in one of the tanks at an oil refinery in Cepu, Central Java, controlled by Indonesia's Energy Ministry, while the facility was shut down for maintenance; one worker died, the fire was extinguished and the ministry opened an investigation, Reuters reported.

Effect on fuel. Impact not assessed.

confirmed Latin America · 18 November 2026

Peru declares eight-month fuel supply emergency over El Nino

On 18 November Peru's Ministry of Energy and Mines declared an eight-month state of emergency for fuel supply, covering processing, refining and storage through to sales, as a preventive measure amid the 2026-2027 El Nino; the ministry warned that heavy rain, floods and landslides could disrupt fuel logistics.

Effect on fuel. The measure is preventive: the ministry says its aim is to preserve continuity of fuel supply.
