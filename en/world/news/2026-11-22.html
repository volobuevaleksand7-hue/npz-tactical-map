confirmed Middle East · 21 November 2026

Gas pipeline fire at Hartha power plant near Basra kills four, injures 35

On 21 November, at least four people were killed and 35 injured in a fire at the Hartha power plant north of Basra, security officials said. According to Iraq's Electricity Ministry, the incident involved a gas pipeline supplying the plant; the fire spread to neighbouring houses and operations at the 800-megawatt station were halted. The cause has not been officially established, and the prime minister ordered an investigation.

Effect on fuel. Damage to the gas pipeline halted the power plant; the impact on fuel supplies and refining has not been assessed.

confirmed Middle East · 21 November 2026

UKMTO reports two tanker attacks off the coast of Oman

On 21 November UKMTO reported that a tanker sailing outbound through the Strait of Hormuz was hit on its stern by an unidentified projectile about 11 nautical miles north of Oman's Khasab, and later reported a second incident in which a tanker was hit in its engine room 17 nautical miles east of Khasab. No casualties were reported, and it remained unclear whether the two incidents involved the same vessel. Iran's navy said an oil tanker caught fire after striking a naval mine in a restricted area east of the strait, accusing the vessel of ignoring warnings; the claim could not be independently verified.

Effect on fuel. The incidents occurred in the Strait of Hormuz, which carried nearly one-fifth of global crude oil before the conflict began; no information on changes in supply volumes has been reported.

confirmed Africa · 20 November 2026

Dangote cuts wholesale petrol price to 1,070 naira per litre, lowest since July

On 20 November, Nigeria's Dangote refinery cut its petrol gantry price by 60 naira to 1,070 naira per litre from 1,130 naira on 18 November, The ICIR reported. The price is the lowest since July 2026, extending a series of November adjustments after a return to EBOB-linked pricing.

Effect on fuel. The wholesale price cut reflects a series of downward November adjustments amid changes in import prices; the impact on retail prices has not been assessed.

confirmed Europe · 20 November 2026

Europe has used 11% of its summer gas stock; storage at lowest level for the date since 2011

On 20 November, APA-Economics, citing Gas Infrastructure Europe, reported that Europe has consumed about 11% of the 51.2 billion cubic metres of gas injected into storage during the summer since the heating season began. Storage was 69.83% full on 18 November, 17.87 percentage points below the five-year average and the lowest level for that date since 2011. Net withdrawals are running 1.7 times above the five-year average.

Effect on fuel. Low inventories and faster withdrawals increase Europe's demand for imported gas and LNG during winter.

confirmed Middle East · 20 November 2026

Iraqi forces take control of Jurf al-Sakhar area and pipeline used by Kataib Hezbollah

On 20 November, Iraqi federal forces deployed a joint command in the Jurf al-Sakhar area of Babil province and began guarding an oil pipeline that Baghdad says Kataib Hezbollah had illegally tapped since 2021, Al-Monitor and OilPrice reported. Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi ordered the creation of the command with army, police and energy units on 19 November. At the same time, Baghdad increased pressure on the Kurdistan Region to hand more oil to federal marketer SOMO.

Effect on fuel. Placing the pipeline under federal guard is intended to stop illegal oil tapping; no confirmed changes in supply volumes have been reported.

confirmed Europe · 20 November 2026

JANAF resumes crude deliveries to Serbia's NIS refinery after two-week outage

On 20 November, Croatian operator JANAF resumed regular crude transportation for Serbia's NIS after repairing the Sisak–Slavonski Brod section, the company said. Pumping had been suspended on 6 November after a pipeline rupture; the spilled oil was recovered and returned to the system. During the outage, Serbia's Pancevo refinery reduced throughput and received crude from state reserves.

Effect on fuel. The resumption restores the sole crude supply route to the Pancevo refinery; throughput had been reduced during the outage.

Iran sets conditions for reopening Strait of Hormuz at talks involving Qatar

On 21 November Iran's Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said reopening the Strait of Hormuz is not difficult if the United States accepts reasonable conditions; according to Deputy Foreign Minister Kazem Gharibabadi, a Qatari delegation held two hours of talks with Iranian representatives in Tehran overnight into 21 November. Gharibabadi said contacts with mediators were continuing but had not yet become formal negotiations, and he did not give details of the conditions.

Effect on fuel. The strait remains of limited accessibility for shipping; the reported talks concern the possibility of reopening it, but no concrete agreement on changing oil supplies has been announced.

reported, single source Europe · 20 November 2026

Norway's October gas sales up nearly 20% as EU storage stays low

On 20 November preliminary data from the Norwegian Offshore Directorate showed that Norway sold 11 billion cubic metres of gas in October, 2.1 billion more than in September; daily sales averaged 355.6 million cubic metres against 296.8 million in September. Almost all Norwegian gas is exported, mostly to Europe by pipeline. According to Gas Infrastructure Europe, EU gas storage was 69.8% full on 18 November, holding about 791 TWh, roughly 12% less than a year earlier. Norwegian crude output rose by 146,000 barrels per day in October to 1.843 million barrels per day.

Effect on fuel. Higher Norwegian supplies partly offset low EU gas storage, which was below year-earlier levels on 18 November, reducing buyers' buffer as heating demand rises.

Tankers carrying Russian diesel bound for the U.S. arrive in Brazil

On 19–20 November, The Maritime Executive reported that three tankers carrying Russian diesel that had been heading to the United States under a deal between Donald Trump and Vladimir Putin arrived in Brazil or diverted toward its coast. According to the outlet, the Leruo and Primorye anchored near Itacoatiara while the Beast was off the South American coast; each carries about 40,000 tonnes of diesel. U.S. buyers declined the cargoes over sanctions risks.

Effect on fuel. The diversion of three diesel cargoes changes supply routes after U.S. buyers declined them; the impact on prices has not been assessed.

reported, single source North America · 20 November 2026

US Mars sour crude turns from record premium to discount as supplies surge

On 20 November Bloomberg reported that the Mars crude grade produced in the Gulf of Mexico moved from a USD 10 premium to West Texas Intermediate to a USD 4 discount in less than a month, the steepest discount since late September, after supplies of heavy, sour crude from Venezuela, Canada and the Middle East rose. At the peak of disruptions in March and April, Mars traded at a record premium of USD 17.50 to WTI; according to Bloomberg, Canada's Trans Mountain pipeline is running at a record 960,000 barrels per day in November, and 34 vessels are being shipped from the Westridge terminal versus 31 in October.

Effect on fuel. According to Bloomberg, cheaper heavy crude lowers feedstock costs for US refiners built for such grades, while heavy oil producers receive lower prices.
