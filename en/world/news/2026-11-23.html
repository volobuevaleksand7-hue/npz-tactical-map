Explosion and fire on the Southern Natural Gas pipeline in Alabama

Late on 21 November (around 23:30 local time) a gas release and fire occurred on a section of the Southern Natural Gas pipeline near Ward in Sumter County, Alabama; flames rose more than 30 metres and were visible tens of kilometres away. There were no injuries and no structures were damaged, and residents of nearby areas were evacuated overnight. The company shut down and depressurised the section, the fire was extinguished and the cause is under investigation. A notice to shippers declared force majeure and cut capacity on the damaged South Main Loop Line east of the York compressor station; according to the company, customer deliveries were not affected. Southern Natural Gas is a roughly 6,900-mile system jointly owned by Kinder Morgan and Southern Company.

Effect on fuel. A section of the interstate gas pipeline is out of service, with force majeure declared and capacity reduced; according to the operator, customer deliveries were not interrupted.

IRGC says two supertankers struck mines in the Strait of Hormuz

On 22 November the navy of Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) said two supertankers sailing on routes Iran has declared off-limits struck naval mines and came to a halt, north of Oman's Al Ghanam Island and east of the Strait of Hormuz. No vessel names, casualty figures or details of the damage were given. Separately, the navy of Iran's regular army said the same day that two oil tankers caught fire after striking mines east of the strait; it was not specified whether the two statements referred to the same vessels. The IRGC also said it destroyed a US MQ-1 Predator drone east of the strait.

Effect on fuel. Impact not assessed.

Houthis claim strike on Rabigh refinery in Saudi Arabia

On 22 November Houthi military spokesman Yahya Sarea announced two operations: a strike on the Rabigh refinery on Saudi Arabia's Red Sea coast and on a radar facility in Wadi ad-Dawasir south of Riyadh. No details were given on the weapons used or the extent of the damage; the Houthis claimed a direct hit, and there is no independent confirmation. The Saudi-led coalition said earlier the same day that it had intercepted and destroyed a drone flying towards the country's western region, which hosts energy facilities; Rabigh is about 165 km north of Jeddah and hosts the Petro Rabigh refinery and petrochemical complex (Saudi Aramco and Sumitomo Chemical).

Effect on fuel. Impact not assessed.
