confirmed Middle East · 15 November 2026

Iran attacks Bahrain base with drones; air defences report intercepts

According to the IRGC, on 15 November its forces launched six drones at the Riffa helicopter base in Bahrain in response to US military action and claimed a successful hit. Bahrain said its air defences intercepted and destroyed the aerial targets; CENTCOM earlier said US forces shot down two drones launched toward Bahrain on Friday. According to Bloomberg, this was the second such episode in three days.

Effect on fuel. Oil steadied after a two-week decline: Brent held near $96 a barrel and WTI traded just below $89, according to Bloomberg.

reported, single source Middle East · 15 November 2026

Iran restricts petrol sales via station fuel cards in 11 provinces

On 15 November station fuel cards, which allowed motorists to buy petrol beyond their individual quota, were withdrawn in 11 Iranian provinces, according to Abdolhossein Hemmati, secretary of parliament's energy committee. He said the restrictions prompted complaints from drivers, and transport operators warned of possible increases in goods prices.

Effect on fuel. The restrictions reduce access to fuel beyond subsidised quotas; transport operators warn of higher costs and goods prices.
