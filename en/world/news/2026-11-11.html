confirmed North America · 10 November 2026

Tanker carrying Russian diesel cargo turned back off the US coast

On 10 November, according to LSEG shipping data and Bloomberg, the Malta-flagged tanker Agioi Fanendes, which loaded 40,000 tonnes of ultra-low sulphur diesel at Primorsk on 22 October, changed course off the US east coast and did not reach New York. Operator Eastmed told Reuters the vessel was awaiting orders and no discharge port had been nominated; the cargo was the first under the October arrangement on Russian diesel supplies to the United States, with 500,000 tonnes planned for November.

Effect on fuel. The delivery of about 40,000 tonnes of Russian diesel to the United States did not take place, as the vessel turned back off the east coast and the announced volume did not reach the US market.

confirmed Europe · 9 November 2026

Remains of seafarers found on tanker Wine Trader in Varna after drone attack

The Varna District Prosecutor's Office in Bulgaria said that severely burned human remains were found while clearing the damaged tanker Wine Trader (6,259 dwt, San Marino-flagged) at a ship repair yard; according to Bulgaria's Interior Ministry, the remains of six of the seven missing seafarers were located and DNA testing has been ordered. The vessel was struck by three drones on 15 October while anchored 13 nautical miles off the coast of Romania, and five crew members were rescued; Romania is leading the investigation.

Effect on fuel. Impact not assessed.

confirmed Middle East · 9 November 2026

Saudi Arabia's East-West oil pipeline attacked in Medina region

On 9 November an official of Saudi Arabia's Energy Ministry said, according to the Saudi Press Agency, that the East-West oil pipeline was targeted in the Medina region: a limited fire broke out and was extinguished, no one was injured and pipeline operations were unaffected. The ministry did not specify whether the line suffered physical damage; no party has claimed responsibility for the attack.

Effect on fuel. According to Al Jazeera, after disruptions to flows through the Strait of Hormuz Saudi Arabia reroutes about 4 million barrels per day of crude through this pipeline to Yanbu, equivalent to roughly 4 percent of global supply.

reported, single source Middle East · 8 November 2026

Egypt cuts gas supplies to fertilizer producers by 33%

On 8 November, according to Reuters, Egyptian fertilizer producers were notified of a new 33% reduction in natural gas supplies, following an earlier 20% cut; Misr Fertilizers Production Company confirmed the notification. According to Reuters sources, the measure aims to curb purchases of expensive LNG, while Egypt is increasing imports of high-sulphur fuel oil: EGPC bought six cargoes for November and December.

Effect on fuel. According to Reuters, amid the gas supply cuts the Egyptian General Petroleum Corporation bought six high-sulphur fuel oil cargoes for November and December, supporting fuel oil demand in the Mediterranean.
