IEA: global oil market to return to surplus in Q4 2026

On 13 November the IEA said in its November report that global oil supply rose by 2.3 million bpd in October to 105.4 million bpd and that the market will return to a surplus in the fourth quarter. The agency also detailed its emergency reserve release: 71 million barrels will be available by the end of February, including 15 million barrels of diesel.

Effect on fuel. According to the IEA, fourth-quarter supply will exceed forecast demand by 1.7 million bpd, while the diesel shortage remains the market's main concern: European diesel prices are holding near USD 180/bbl.

confirmed North America · 12 November 2026

Trump says US does not need a deal with Iran, refers to control of Strait of Hormuz

On 12 November US President Donald Trump said Washington does not need a deal with Iran and that the United States controls the Strait of Hormuz. The statement was reported by OilPrice and Middle East Monitor; details of possible talks were not disclosed.

Effect on fuel. Oil prices rose by more than 1% after the statement as the market reacted to the lack of prospects for a Hormuz deal, Middle East Monitor reported.

reported, single source Middle East · 12 November 2026

Houthis claim strikes on East-West pipeline and Yanbu facilities on 12 November

On 12 November Houthi military spokesman Yahya Saree said the group struck a pumping station on the East-West pipeline with a drone and fired a ballistic missile at oil facilities in Yanbu. Saudi Arabia did not confirm any damage, and neither Aramco nor the Saudi Press Agency issued statements; the same day the Saudi-led coalition reported intercepting suspected drones over the Riyadh region and the Eastern Province.

Effect on fuel. The East-West pipeline and the Yanbu terminals are Saudi Arabia's main export route bypassing Hormuz; according to Riyadh, about 4 million barrels per day are rerouted through it, roughly 4% of global supply.
