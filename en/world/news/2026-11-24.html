confirmed Middle East · 24 November 2026

Hormuz oil traffic falls 40% amid tanker attacks

According to AIS ship-tracking data cited by Al Jazeera on 24 November, the flow of oil through the Strait of Hormuz has fallen by 40%; in the previous 24 hours 28 ships crossed the strait — 12 into the Gulf and 16 out of it. The outlet reported that more than 25 tankers were hit in the past week, with Brent trading around $88.35 a barrel.

Effect on fuel. The drop in transits restricts seaborne oil supplies from the Gulf and deepens the disruption Al Jazeera describes as a global energy crisis.

confirmed Europe · 23 November 2026

European gas prices jump 8% on cold snap and low inventories

On 23 November European gas prices rose more than 8% at the Amsterdam open, and on 24 November the TTF front-month price climbed to $98.77 (90.50 euros) per MWh amid forecasts of colder weather. According to Gas Infrastructure Europe, EU gas storage was 68.9% full on 23 November versus a five-year average of 89%; the UK, Germany and France expect freezing temperatures this week.

Effect on fuel. Faster withdrawals from record-low seasonal inventories raise the risk of shortages in the coldest winter months.

confirmed Middle East · 23 November 2026

U.S. Treasury expands sanctions on Iran's shadow banking network

On 23 November the U.S. Treasury announced sanctions on more than two dozen Iranian exchange houses, front companies and key individuals as part of a campaign it describes as targeting Iran's shadow banking networks; Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent called the measures part of efforts to cut off financial channels for Iran's oil revenue. According to CNBC, the move came amid a widening naval blockade of Iranian ports.

Effect on fuel. The sanctions target financial channels serving Iran's oil revenue and may complicate payments for Iranian oil supplies.
