Trump says the US will keep the naval blockade of the Strait of Hormuz; says Qatar and other Gulf states prefer the status quo

On 10 November Trump told Reuters and at a White House briefing that the United States would for now maintain the naval blockade of the Strait of Hormuz. According to him, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Bahrain and Kuwait prefer the status quo, meaning continuation of the blockade without new strikes. The positions he attributes to these states come only from Trump; the states themselves have not confirmed them in the available materials.
