EU sanctions теневой флот owners, managers and insurers

On 16 November the EU announced a new sanctions package against shipowners, managers, insurers and financial networks supporting Russia's теневой флот. The list adds 12 individuals and 101 entities, including companies based in the UAE, Oman, Seychelles and China that Brussels says are involved in transporting or trading Russian oil.

reported, single source Europe · 16 November 2026

Cargo ship BETUL 8 sinks off Bulgaria; crew reports sea drone attack

On 16 November the Somali-flagged cargo ship BETUL 8 sank about 40 nautical miles southeast of Cape Kaliakra in Bulgaria's exclusive economic zone; all 12 crew members were rescued with the involvement of vessels from Bulgaria, Romania and Turkey. According to Bulgarian outlet Maritime.bg, citing survivors, two unmanned surface vessels struck the ship. Bulgaria's transport ministry did not confirm the cause; the vessel was sailing from Romania's Tulcea to Egypt with a cargo of wheat.

Effect on fuel. Impact not assessed.

Bahrain says it intercepted Iranian strikes; IRGC claims attack on US radar

According to Iranian state television, the IRGC carried out a drone attack on a US radar system at a base in Bahrain overnight into 15 November. Bahrain said it intercepted the Iranian strikes; on 16 November US Secretary of State Marco Rubio condemned the attacks in a call with Bahrain's foreign minister, and Manama demanded an immediate halt to the strikes at a UN conference. There is no independent confirmation of damage to the radar.
