Kuwait-flagged tanker Al Dasma struck by unidentified projectile off Bukha, Oman, near the Strait of Hormuz

According to UKMTO (warning 179-26), at about 01:45 UTC on 21 October the Kuwait-flagged tanker Al Dasma was hit by an unidentified projectile roughly 11 nautical miles west of Bukha, Oman, near the Strait of Hormuz. A fire broke out and was extinguished; the vessel is now at anchor closer to Bukha. The crew, including 21 Indian seafarers, is reported safe and no environmental impact has been identified. The perpetrator has not been established.

Effect on fuel. Supply impact not assessed; the incident raises risks for petroleum product shipping through the Strait of Hormuz.

Russia announces first diesel cargo to the US after sanctions easing

On 21 October Deputy Prime Minister Alexander Novak, as reported by Reuters, said Russia would send its first diesel tanker (about 40,000 tonnes) to the United States; the paperwork is nearing completion and further shipments are to follow. Departure is planned for 22 October; the port and the actual sailing have not been independently confirmed. The announcement follows Russia lifting its self-imposed diesel export ban on 9 October and a temporary US authorization for Russian diesel shipments.

Effect on fuel. Russian diesel exports resume after the ban; analysts expect a limited effect on global prices.

Panama-flagged tanker struck by unidentified projectile off Iraq's coast, according to UKMTO

According to UKMTO, a tanker flying the Panamanian flag was hit by an unidentified projectile about 28–30 nautical miles south-east of Al Faw, Iraq. Reports differ on damage: one publication cites hull damage, another reports none. The crew is reported safe and no environmental impact has been recorded; the perpetrator is unidentified and the investigation is ongoing.

Effect on fuel. Impact not assessed.
