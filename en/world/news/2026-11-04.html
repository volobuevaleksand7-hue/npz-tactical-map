Sri Lanka: queues at filling stations as CPC petrol deliveries are delayed

According to The Morning, long queues formed at filling stations in Colombo and other areas of Sri Lanka due to a shortfall of petrol at Ceylon Petroleum Corporation (CPC) stations. The Fuel Dealers' Association said some stations had not received petrol from CPC since Thursday and were forced to close their pumps; according to a union official, the next petrol stock was scheduled to arrive on 8 November. The scale of the shortage is not specified in the report.

Effect on fuel. Some filling stations suspended petrol sales because CPC deliveries had not arrived; according to the report, the next stock was expected on 8 November.

Four sabotage blasts halt northern Iraqi crude exports to Ceyhan

According to an oil official, reported on 23 October, four blasts hit the main gathering centre for at least four northern Iraqi fields (Kirkuk, Janbour, Bay Hassan, Khabaz and others), completely halting crude exports to Turkey's Ceyhan port. Repairs are estimated to take up to one month. The perpetrator and independent confirmation are not identified in the available reporting.

Effect on fuel. Crude exports from northern Iraq to Ceyhan have been completely halted; according to the official, repairs may take up to one month.

Sri Lanka private distributors cut supplies amid price surge

Three private distributors (LIOC, Sinopec, RM Parks) reduced shipments of gasoline and diesel citing unprofitability at current prices; the government mandated minimum stock levels, and CPC's share of diesel supply rose to 82%, worsening local filling station shortages according to Daily Mirror.

Effect on fuel. Reduction of private supplier deliveries shifts the burden onto state-owned CPC and intensifies diesel shortage at local filling stations.
